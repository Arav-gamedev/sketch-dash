How to play: a word appears above the canvas — draw it before the 60-second timer runs out. No letters or numbers, just your best doodle.
  1. Hit New word to get a prompt and start the clock. Stuck? Tap Get a hint for a tip on how to sketch it.
  2. Draw it. If someone else is guessing, tap Hide word so it doesn't give it away on their screen.
  3. When you're done, tap Rate my drawing for a score out of 10.
  4. Tap Got it! once it's guessed (or you're happy with it) — that's a point and your streak grows. Skip if you're stuck.
  5. Happy with your sketch? Tap Save to gallery to keep it below.
